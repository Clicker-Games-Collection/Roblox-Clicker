## How to Play

In **Roblox Clicker**, the gameplay centers around continuous clicking. Tap on the Robux icon to generate currency, then use your earnings to buy upgrades from the shop. Each upgrade boosts your Robux generation, helping you accumulate wealth more quickly and unlock a range of achievements.

## Game Controls

- **Mouse**: Click to interact with game elements.
- **Mobile**: Tap the screen to play.
